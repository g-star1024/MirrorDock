Everything your phone does, on your computer

Mirroring, control, audio, screenshots, recordings and file transfer all happen in one window — no more switching between devices.

Mirror and control, with a full shortcut set

Your phone's screen appears live in a computer window, and your mouse and keyboard control the phone. Shortcuts work even when the mirror window has focus — no need to return to the main app.

⌃ / ⌘ + Alt + S ⌃ / ⌘ + Alt + R ⌃ / ⌘ + Alt + D

By default: screenshot, toggle recording, rotate display. Change the modifiers in Settings → Global shortcuts.

Inside the mirror window there's a whole set:

Modifier + H Home + B Back + S Recents + N Notifications + P Power + O Screen off + ↑ ↓ Volume + F Fullscreen + Q Quit mirroring

The modifier is customizable too.

  • Connect by cable or over Wi-Fi

    Plug in a cable and go, or connect over the same Wi-Fi. Android 11+ supports the native "Pair devices using Wi-Fi" flow: the computer shows a QR code, the phone scans it, pairing and connection complete automatically.

  • One card per phone

    The connection page merges by device: USB, wireless, or stale offline entries produce one card per phone, with state as the action. Revoke authorization and clear records from your computer in one click.

  • Screen orientation follows your phone

    Orientation is unlocked by default. Open a landscape game or video and the picture rotates with the window resized to match. You can also lock a specific angle manually.

  • Hear your phone on your computer

    Android 11+ can forward system audio, muting the phone locally so only your computer's speakers play it.

  • Screenshots and recording

    Screenshots go to Pictures / MirrorDock and recordings to Videos / MirrorDock. The mirror window writes the file directly and waits for finalization before exiting, so recordings always play.

  • Transfer files both ways

    Sent files land in Download / MirrorDock on the phone; retrieved files go to your computer's Downloads folder. Only USB or LAN is used. The desktop notifies you instantly when the phone sends something, and you can also drag a file into the MirrorDock window.

  • Media library with thumbnails and quick view

    Reads all photos and videos from the phone's system media index, with type filters and filename/folder search. Photos show thumbnails and open full-size; videos use a cover placeholder and open in your system player. Thumbnails are cached only on this computer, retrieved files go to your Downloads folder, and phone media is read-only — never modified, deleted or uploaded.

  • Phone notifications on your computer — and reply from there

    Enable Notification mirroring in the companion app (off by default, needs your explicit consent). New notifications appear live under Tools on the desktop; notifications with a reply field can be answered right there, with text sent back to the phone over an encrypted channel. Only paired computers receive them; nothing is written to disk or logs, and you can turn it off anytime.

  • Mirror several phones at once

    Each phone gets its own mirror window. The tray lists and ends them individually, settings can target one device, and frequently used devices can be pinned. Desktop mode and the app launched into the virtual display are remembered per device, with an app-name dropdown instead of package names.

  • Automatic reconnect (USB and Wi-Fi)

    Cable unplugged, or the phone dropped off Wi-Fi after sleeping? The connection page tells you it is waiting and restores mirroring automatically when the cable is back or the phone reconnects (up to 15 minutes). The picture returns the moment the phone wakes.

  • Type like a local keyboard

    Your computer keyboard connects as a hardware keyboard, so no fullscreen soft keyboard appears. MirrorDock switches to a usable layout when mirroring starts and restores your IME afterwards. Enter sends messages directly in chat apps.

  • In-app automatic updates

    Check and upgrade in one click under Settings → About. Updates are signature-verified so a tampered installer never runs.

  • Adjustable frame rate and quality

    Beyond three quality levels, you can cap the frame rate at 60 / 30 / 24 — cooler on older phones and kinder to battery on long sessions.

  • Install APKs

    Pick an installer on your computer or drag an .apk into the MirrorDock window. Reinstalling keeps app data, and the phone still asks you to confirm.

  • Companion app, encrypted pairing

    Install the companion app to establish a TLS 1.3 connection with a pinned certificate fingerprint. With the always-on channel enabled you can reconnect without scanning; apps and files both travel over this encrypted channel.

Three steps to start mirroring

Set up your phone once; after that, just connect.

  1. 1

    Enable debugging on your phone

    Open Developer options and enable USB debugging; for wireless, also enable Wireless debugging. The app includes per-brand path guidance; menu names follow what your phone actually shows.

  2. 2

    Connect the computer to your phone

    Plug in the cable and click Recheck on the computer. For wireless, generate a pairing QR under Wireless connection and scan it with the phone's "Pair devices using Wi-Fi".

  3. 3

    Start mirroring

    Click Start mirroring and your phone appears in the window. Screenshots, recording, file transfer and rotation all happen there.

What it can't do

This matters more than the feature list. Remote-control tools are most vague right here, so we state the boundaries plainly before you find out the hard way.

  • × No bypassing the lock screen, no unlocking MirrorDock cannot wake a locked phone or enter the system. It can only take over the screen after you unlock; preventing auto-lock during mirroring is an option you enable in advance.
  • × Protected content shows as black Android itself blocks payment pages, password entry, banking and streaming apps — both display and screenshots are black. That is platform behavior, not a fault.
  • × No unattended remote control No silent background connection and no persistence that bypasses authorization. Unplug the cable or revoke debugging access and the connection ends.
  • ✓ No video, clipboard or passwords in logs Diagnostics are previewed before export, with device serials, pairing codes and file paths stripped. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
  • ✓ The free tier includes all mirroring features Screenshots, file transfer, audio forwarding and shortcuts are all free. Pro only adds MP4 recording. The beta bundles the activation code, so there is nothing to type in.

FAQ

Grouped by topic — start here when something goes wrong.

Why does USB debugging need to be on? Is it safe?

It is Android's official developer channel, and MirrorDock uses it to gain screen and control access. Authorization applies only to the computer you confirmed. You can revoke it any time under Developer options → Revoke USB debugging authorizations, and the connection ends immediately. MirrorDock cannot and will not bypass lock screens or crack apps.

Wireless connection drops or won't connect?

Every time you re-enable Wireless debugging the port changes and you must pair again — that is expected. Before connecting, make sure the phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi without client isolation. On Android 11+, use QR pairing: the computer discovers the address automatically, no manual copying.

No sound on the computer, or the phone still plays it?

Audio forwarding requires Android 11+. Calls, some apps, and copyright-protected content may be blocked by system capture policy. The phone must also be unlocked when mirroring starts. Once enabled, sound plays only on the computer and the phone is muted.

My game didn't switch to landscape automatically?

Make sure Settings → Display orientation is set to Auto (follow phone). Choosing a fixed angle locks the picture to that angle on the computer and stops following the phone.

macOS says the developer can't be verified, or Windows shows SmartScreen?

The beta is not code-signed or notarized, so both systems warn. On macOS choose Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security; on Windows click More info → Run anyway. The stable release will be signed.

Does the companion app crash when scanning a QR code?

The crash on the scan page was fixed in v0.1.2. If it still happens, the companion app shows a red card with the last exit reason and stack trace — screenshot it and open an Issue.
